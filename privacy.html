Privacy Policy

New School BJJ Brixton
Last updated: 16 October 2026

New School BJJ Brixton ("NSBJJ", "we", "us") is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu gym in Brixton, London, run by Reiss Bailey. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. What data we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone number when you book a trial, fill in a form or contact us.
  • Payment details: when you pay for a trial or membership. Card payments are processed by our payment provider (such as Stripe); we do not store full card numbers.
  • Membership and enquiry information: classes booked, attendance, membership status and messages you send us.
  • Advertising and website data: if you interact with our Facebook or Instagram ads or our website, Meta may collect data through its ads platform and the Meta Pixel, such as pages visited, actions taken (for example submitting a form), device and browser information and IP address.

2. Why we use it (and our lawful basis)

  • To run memberships and trials: booking, payments, attendance and account management (contract).
  • To contact enquirers: replying to your enquiry and following up about trials and classes (legitimate interests, or consent where required).
  • To advertise: measuring and improving our ads and showing relevant ads, including through the Meta Pixel (consent, where required for cookies and similar technologies, otherwise legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal obligations: such as keeping financial records.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the gym, including:

  • Payment providers such as Stripe, to process payments.
  • CRM and communication providers such as GoHighLevel, to manage enquiries, bookings and messages.
  • Advertising providers such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to run and measure our ads.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where required by law.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Enquiry and trial data: up to 2 years after our last contact with you, unless you become a member.
  • Membership data: for the length of your membership and up to 2 years after it ends.
  • Financial records: 6 years, as required by UK law.
  • Advertising data held by Meta: according to Meta's own retention policies.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (the "right to erasure");
  • restrict or object to how we use your data, including for direct marketing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To make a request, including a request to delete your data, email us at the address below. We will respond within one month. You can also unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time using the link or instructions in the message.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
