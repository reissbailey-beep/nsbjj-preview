FAQ

Good
questions.

Anything else? Call 07578 284838.

I've never trained. Can I start?

Yes. Our beginners' classes are made for complete beginners. You'll warm up as a group, learn the technique of the day, and spar at the end at your own pace. Nobody is ever forced to do anything they don't want to, and you're welcome to watch your first session if you'd prefer.

How does the £20 trial month work?

You get a full month of unlimited adult classes for £20. Start your trial here, then book your classes in the Gymdesk app. After the month, memberships are £70 a month for 2 classes a week or £95 a month unlimited.

What should I wear?

Comfortable sportswear is fine. If you have a gi, wear it. If not, you can buy one or rent one for a session at the gym. Bring a water bottle and a towel.

What ages are the kids' classes?

Cubs are 4–7 and Juniors are 8–12. Your child's first class is free: book it online. After that it's £75 a month for your first child, with 20% off for each brother or sister.

Is BJJ OK for women?

Absolutely. Women train in every class on the timetable, from beginners' sessions to advanced.

Do I need to book classes?

Yes, through the Gymdesk app or the online timetable. If a class is full, join the waiting list and you'll be told if a space opens up.
